Graduation day at Restore Education was nothing short of powerful. The auditorium buzzed with pride as families filled the seats to watch their loved ones cross the stage, many of them seeing this moment years in the making. Keynote speaker Dr. Adriana Rocha Garcia captured the heart of the day when she reminded graduates that everyone deserves a second chance:

"Everyone gets a do-over, and today might be your do-over. Today might be the first day in your second chance."

— Dr. Adriana Rocha Garcia, keynote speaker

That message resonated throughout the room, echoed by graduates who shared what earning their GED and diploma meant to them. Again and again, they spoke about their gratitude for the opportunity to finish what they started and the joy of having their families there to witness it. The energy was moving and unmistakable: this was more than a ceremony, it was a room full of people stepping into new beginnings.
